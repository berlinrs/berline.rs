**Rust Berlin Talks** is back for another evening of talks, shared ideas, and networking.

Join us to learn, share, ask, and connect. If you work with Rust professionally, use it in side projects, contribute to open source, or are simply curious to learn more, you are very welcome.

## Agenda

- Doors open: 18:30
- Start: 19:00
- Talks
- Lightning talks
- Networking

## Speakers

- Egor Lebedev - Welcome and a short note from the RustRover team
- Iván Ovejero - Compiling Rust Ideas into Go: Learnings from Lisette
- Orhun Parmaksız - A New Way Into the Terminal

Interested in speaking at a future Rust Berlin Talks event? Send us your proposal by [email](mailto:liran.aloni@jetbrains.com) or reach out on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/liranaloni/).

## Speaker Bios

Iván Ovejero is a Senior Software Engineer on the Core Platform team at n8n. He is the author of [Lisette](https://lisette.run), a language inspired by Rust that compiles to Go. His interests include type systems, compilers, and developer tooling.

Orhun Parmaksız is a Rust Developer Advocate at JetBrains and the creator of [Ratatui](https://ratatui.rs).

## Open mic & future talks

We'll reserve a few short slots for anyone who wants to share something with the group. This can be a quick idea, a project, or an experience. Slides are optional.

If you're interested in giving a longer talk at a future meetup, this is also a good opportunity to connect with the organizers.
